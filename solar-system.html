Orbits use each body's real eccentricity and inclination. The Sun lies at each ellipse's focus, not its center. The asteroid belt, Kuiper belt, and dashed Oort Cloud boundary are interactive. Distances and sizes are compressed to fit on screen. For the gas and ice giants, the mean temperature refers to the average cloud-top temperature (near the 1-bar pressure level), as these planets have no solid surface.
