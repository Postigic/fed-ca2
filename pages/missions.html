Milestones in Space Exploration

1957

Sputnik 1

+

First artificial satellite.

Launch Date: 4 October 1957

Country: Soviet Union

Rocket: Sputnik 8K71PS

Mass: 83.6 kg

Sputnik 1 orbited Earth every 96 minutes and marked the beginning of the Space Age.

1969

Apollo 11

+

First crewed Moon landing.

Launch Date: 16 July 1969

Landing: 20 July 1969

Crew: Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, Michael Collins

Neil Armstrong became the first person to walk on the Moon while Buzz Aldrin followed shortly after.

1998

International Space Station

+

Largest space laboratory.

First Module: Zarya

Partners: NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, JAXA, CSA

The ISS has continuously hosted astronauts since November 2000 and serves as a research laboratory.

2022

Artemis I

+

First Artemis mission.

Launch: 16 November 2022

Spacecraft: Orion

Mission Duration: 25.5 days

Artemis I successfully tested the Orion spacecraft before future crewed missions to the Moon.
